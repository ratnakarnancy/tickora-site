Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026 · Applies to Tickora Daily for iPhone, version 1.0 and later

In short
  • Tickora stores your information on your iPhone.
  • If you turn on iCloud Backup, an encrypted copy is also kept in your own iCloud Drive. It's off unless you turn it on.
  • The app has no account, no servers of ours, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party code.
  • Tickora never sends your information to us. We can't see it.

Who we are

Tickora Daily is published by Tickora. On your iPhone Home Screen the app appears as “Tickora”, and this policy uses that shorter name. Questions about this policy: support.tickora@gmail.com.

Information you enter

Tickora saves what you enter: deadlines and renewals, appointments, vehicles and other assets, loans and payments, medicines and the doses you record, daily routines, notes and checklists, events and guest lists, and any documents or photos you attach. It also saves your settings, such as your reminder time.

This is kept in Tickora's private storage on your iPhone and protected by iOS Data Protection. Records can't be read after your iPhone restarts until you unlock it once; attached documents can't be read while your iPhone is locked.

Tickora has no servers and contains no code that connects to the internet itself. Your information leaves your iPhone only when you choose: if you turn on iCloud Backup, iOS uploads Tickora's encrypted backups to your iCloud Drive; if you add an appointment to Apple Calendar, Calendar handles it; and if you share or export something, it goes where you send it.

Please don't store passport numbers, card numbers, account numbers, or passwords in Tickora. The app is not designed as a secure vault for that kind of information.

iCloud Backup

iCloud Backup is optional. When enabled, Tickora creates an encrypted backup on your iPhone and saves it to Tickora's private folder in your iCloud Drive. Each backup contains your records and their attached documents and photos, and is encrypted by Tickora before it is uploaded. You can open a backup using your recovery key or, if you have set one, your recovery passphrase. The app stores your recovery key and the key derived from your passphrase (never the passphrase itself) in your iPhone's Keychain. If iCloud Keychain is enabled, these keys can sync to your other Apple devices; Apple protects iCloud Keychain data with end-to-end encryption. Tickora's developer never receives your backups or recovery keys and operates no backup server. Tickora does not sync your live records between devices. Automatic backups run approximately once a day, and Tickora keeps the three most recent backups. You can turn off iCloud Backup or manage your backups in Settings → Backup & Data → iCloud Backup & Restore. iCloud services are provided by Apple and are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications and alarms

If you allow notifications, Tickora schedules reminders with iOS on your iPhone; no server is involved. If you allow alarms (iOS 26 or later), the reminders you choose ring like the Clock app and can show on the Lock Screen while they ring.

A reminder shows the item's title and when it's due, and sometimes one short detail, such as an appointment's location or a loan payment's estimated amount. It never includes your notes or medicine instructions. To keep titles off the Lock Screen, turn on Settings → Hide titles in notifications.

You can turn notifications off anytime in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Tickora.

Home Screen widgets

If you add a Tickora widget, the app keeps a small copy of what's coming up (titles, dates and the kind of item, never notes) in storage on your iPhone that only Tickora and its widgets can read, protected the same way. Widgets show those titles on your Home Screen, so anyone who can see your unlocked iPhone can see them. With Hide titles or App Lock turned on, widgets show only the kind of item, such as “Appointment”.

Permissions Tickora may ask for

Each is optional and asked for only when you use the feature.

  • Apple Calendar. When you add an appointment to Apple Calendar or choose to see your calendar in Tickora, Tickora asks for calendar access. It reads events only to show them on your iPhone, and changes or removes only events it added. Events added to Apple Calendar follow your calendar account, so if that calendar syncs (for example through iCloud), the event syncs too.
  • Contacts. Adding guests opens Apple's contact picker. Tickora gets only the contacts you pick (name, phone, email), copied into that event. It never sees the rest of your contacts.
  • Camera. Used to scan a document so Tickora can read its dates. The text is read on your iPhone; the scan isn't saved unless you attach it.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition. Tickora accesses the microphone only when you tap the microphone button in Ask Tickora. Speech is converted into text using on-device recognition. Audio is not stored, and Tickora does not transmit voice recordings or transcripts to its servers or third-party speech-recognition services. If on-device recognition is unavailable, voice input will not work, but text input remains available.
  • Photos. You choose photos in Apple's photo picker; Tickora receives only those.
  • Face ID or Touch ID. Used for App Lock. iOS checks it and tells Tickora only whether it succeeded. Tickora never sees or stores your face, fingerprint or passcode.

Spotlight, Siri and Ask Tickora

Spotlight is off unless you turn it on in Settings. When on, iOS indexes the titles and dates of deadlines, appointments, assets and loans on your iPhone so they appear in search. Turning it off removes them.

Siri and Shortcuts. If you use Tickora's Siri phrases or Shortcuts actions, Tickora reads your records on your iPhone to answer, and Siri shows or speaks the answer. While App Lock is on, Tickora doesn't answer with your records. Your spoken request is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Ask Tickora works on your iPhone. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, it uses Apple's on-device model only; Apple's Private Cloud Compute is never used. Voice input in Ask Tickora is processed on your iPhone using on-device speech recognition.

What we don't collect

We do not collect, receive, or have access to anything you keep in Tickora, including your iCloud Backup, your contacts, your location, device identifiers or usage data. Tickora does not track you across apps or websites.

Information handled by Apple

Some data is handled by Apple, not by Tickora, under Apple's Privacy Policy:

  • Device backups. Tickora's data, including its recovery points, is part of your iPhone's own backups (to iCloud or your computer) if those are on. This is separate from Tickora's optional iCloud Backup above.
  • Crash reports. If you have chosen in iOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may share anonymous crash reports for Tickora with us. These contain technical information about the crash, not your records.
  • App Store. Downloads and purchases go through Apple's App Store.

Deleting your data

  • To delete one item, open it and choose Delete.
  • Delete All Data (Tickora Settings) removes every record and attached document from this iPhone, with all scheduled reminders and alarms, the widgets' copy and Spotlight entries. Tickora first saves a recovery point on this iPhone so you can undo it from Recovery Points.
  • Delete All Data and Recovery Points does the same and also removes every recovery point. This can't be undone.
  • Neither option deletes your iCloud backups. To delete a backup, open Settings → Backup & Data → iCloud Backup & Restore and swipe left on the backup.
  • Events Tickora added to Apple Calendar stay there unless you turn on Also remove Tickora's events from Apple Calendar when deleting.
  • Your settings, such as reminder time and Hide titles, are kept.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone. Your iCloud Backup stays in iCloud until you delete it (iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage). Copies may also remain in earlier device backups until they're replaced.

Children

Tickora is a general-audience utility. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes

If a future version handles data differently (for example, optional syncing), we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
