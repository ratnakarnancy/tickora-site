Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026 · Applies to Tickora Daily for iPhone, version 1.0

In short
  • Tickora stores your deadlines only on your iPhone.
  • The app has no account, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party code.
  • Tickora does not send your information to us or anyone else.

Who we are

Tickora Daily is published by Tickora. On your iPhone Home Screen the app appears as “Tickora”, and this policy uses that shorter name. Questions about this policy: support.tickora@gmail.com.

Information you enter

When you add a deadline, Tickora saves what you type — title, category, due date, optional notes, reminder choices — plus the dates it was created, changed, or completed. This is saved in a file inside the app's private storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS Data Protection: it can't be read after your iPhone restarts until you unlock it for the first time.

Your chosen reminder time is saved in the app's settings on the device.

None of this leaves your iPhone through Tickora. The app contains no code that connects to the internet.

Please don't store passport numbers, card numbers, account numbers, or passwords in Tickora. The app is not designed as a secure vault for that kind of information.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Tickora schedules reminders using iOS's local notification system. They are created and delivered on your iPhone; no server is involved. A reminder shows the deadline's title and when it's due. It never includes your notes. Like any notification, it can appear on your lock screen depending on your iOS settings.

You can turn notifications off anytime in Settings → Notifications → Tickora.

Home Screen widget

If you add Tickora's widget, the app keeps a small copy of your active deadlines (title, category and due date, never notes) in a storage area on your iPhone that only Tickora and its widget can read. It is protected by iOS Data Protection in the same way. The widget shows those titles and dates on your Home Screen, so anyone who can see your unlocked Home Screen can see them. Remove the widget if you'd rather they not be visible.

What we don't collect

We do not collect, receive, or have access to your deadlines, notes, reminders, contacts, location, device identifiers, usage data, or any other personal information. Tickora does not track you across apps or websites.

Information handled by Apple

Some data is handled by Apple, not by Tickora, under Apple's Privacy Policy:

  • Backups. Tickora's data is included in your iPhone's backups (to iCloud or your computer) if you have backups turned on.
  • Crash reports. If you have chosen in iOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may share anonymous crash reports for Tickora with us. These contain technical information about the crash, not your deadlines.
  • App Store. Downloads and purchases go through Apple's App Store.

Deleting your data

  • To delete one deadline, open it and tap Delete Deadline.
  • To delete everything, open Tickora's Settings and tap Delete All Data. This removes every deadline, any unreadable copy the app set aside after a storage error, every scheduled reminder, and the widget's copy, and resets the reminder time.
  • Deleting the app also deletes its data from your iPhone. Copies may remain in earlier device backups until those are replaced.

Children

Tickora is a general-audience utility. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes

If a future version handles data differently (for example, optional syncing), we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
