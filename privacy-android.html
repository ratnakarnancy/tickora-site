Privacy Policy for Android

Effective November 18, 2026 · Applies to Tickora Daily for Android, version 0.1.0 and later · For the iPhone app, see the iPhone Privacy Policy

In short
  • Tickora stores your information on your phone.
  • The Android app doesn't ask for internet access, so it can't send your information anywhere by itself.
  • The app has no account, no servers of ours, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking or advertising code.
  • Tickora never sends your information to us. We can't see it.

Who we are

Tickora Daily is published by Tickora. On your phone the app appears as “Tickora”, and this policy uses that shorter name. Questions about this policy: support.tickora@gmail.com.

Information you enter

Tickora saves what you enter: deadlines and renewals, appointments and meetings, vehicles, properties and other assets, loans and payments, rentals, medicines and the doses you record, daily routines, notes and checklists, events and guest lists, items you capture in the Smart Inbox, and any documents, photos or scans you attach. It also saves your settings, such as your reminder time.

This is kept in Tickora's private storage on your phone, which other apps can't read. On phones with Android's built-in storage encryption (standard on phones that launched with Android 10 or later), it is encrypted along with the rest of your phone's data, and after your phone restarts it can't be read until you unlock it once. So that reminders still arrive after a restart, Tickora keeps a short list of upcoming reminder times that can be read before you unlock. It holds only identifiers, times and the kind of reminder, never titles or other details, and reminders shown before you unlock use generic text.

The Android app does not request the internet permission and contains no code that connects to the internet. Your information leaves your phone only when you choose: if you export a backup, you choose where the file goes; and if you share or export something, such as an event invitation or a guest list, it goes where you send it.

Please don't store passport numbers, card numbers, account numbers, or passwords in Tickora. The app is not designed as a secure vault for that kind of information.

Backup and restore

Tickora has no online backup. In Settings → Backup & Data you can create an encrypted backup file. You choose a password, Tickora encrypts the file on your phone with that password, and Android's file picker lets you choose where to save it, for example on your phone or in a storage app you use. The file contains your records and their attached documents and photos. Tickora doesn't keep your password, and the developer never receives your backups or passwords. If you save the file to a cloud storage app, that service's own privacy policy applies to it.

To restore, you choose a backup file with Android's file picker and enter its password. Restoring replaces what is currently in Tickora; your current data is saved as a recovery point first.

Recovery points are automatic copies of your records (without attached documents) kept in Tickora's private storage on your phone, so you can undo a change, a restore or Delete All Data. Tickora makes one a minute after you change something, once a day, and before any restore, and keeps the seven most recent. They never leave your phone.

Tickora's data is excluded from Android's own backup and from device-to-device transfer, so it isn't copied to your Google account backup or to a new phone. To move to a new phone, export an encrypted backup and restore it there.

Notifications and alarms

If you allow notifications, Tickora schedules reminders with Android on your phone; no server is involved. If you choose a reminder style that rings, Tickora uses Android's exact alarms so it rings on time, can show the alarm over the Lock Screen while it rings, and runs a foreground service only while the alarm is ringing. Tickora also sets its reminders again after your phone restarts or the time changes.

A reminder shows the item's title and when it's due, and sometimes one short detail, such as an appointment's location or a loan payment's amount. Optional Smart Insights notifications, off unless you turn them on, sum up what needs attention, at most once a day. Notifications never include your notes or medicine instructions. To keep titles off the Lock Screen, turn on Settings → Privacy & Security → Hide titles in notifications; reminders then use generic text, such as “Scheduled reminder”.

You can turn notifications and alarms off anytime in your phone's Settings → Apps → Tickora.

Permissions Tickora may ask for

Each is optional and asked for only when you use the feature. Tickora works without them, though reminders can't alert you without notifications.

  • Notifications. To show reminders. On Android 13 and later, Android asks you first.
  • Alarms and reminders, and full-screen alarms. To ring reminders on time and show a ringing alarm over the Lock Screen, like a clock app. Only used for reminders you set to ring.
  • Camera. Used only when you scan a document. Pages are kept on your phone, and saved only if you choose to keep the scan. Tickora doesn't read text from scans or photos.
  • Photos and files. You choose photos with Android's photo picker and PDFs or backup files with Android's file picker. Tickora receives only the items you pick and doesn't need access to the rest of your photos or files.
  • Fingerprint, face or screen lock. Used for App Lock. Android checks it and tells Tickora only whether it succeeded. Tickora never sees or stores your fingerprint, face or screen lock. While App Lock is on, Tickora is blank in recent apps and can't be captured in screenshots.

Tickora also uses permissions that Android grants without asking, to work in the background on your phone: to start after a restart so reminders are set again, to vibrate, and to keep the phone awake briefly while a reminder or alarm is delivered. Tickora does not ask for your location, contacts, microphone, calendar, or internet access.

Smart Inbox and Ask Tickora

Smart Inbox keeps what you capture (typed or pasted text, photos, PDFs and scans) on your phone. Typed text is read on your phone to suggest dates and details; nothing becomes a record until you confirm it.

Ask Tickora works on your phone using Tickora's built-in rules. It doesn't use an AI model or any online service, and this version has no voice input. It answers only from what you've saved, and shows every change for you to confirm before anything is saved.

What we don't collect

We do not collect, receive, or have access to anything you keep in Tickora, including your backups, your location, device identifiers or usage data. Tickora does not track you across apps or websites.

Information handled by Google

Some data is handled by Google, not by Tickora, under Google's Privacy Policy:

  • Crash reports. If you have chosen in your phone's settings to share usage and diagnostics data, Google may provide us with anonymous crash and performance reports for Tickora through Google Play. These contain technical information about the app, not your records.
  • Google Play. Downloads, updates and testing programs go through Google Play.

Deleting your data

  • To delete one item, open it and choose Delete.
  • Delete All Data (Settings → Backup & Data) removes every record and attached document from this phone, with all scheduled reminders and alarms. Tickora first saves a recovery point on this phone so you can undo it from Recovery Points (attached documents can't be brought back).
  • Delete All Data and Recovery Points does the same and also removes every recovery point. This can't be undone.
  • Neither option deletes backup files you exported. Delete those where you saved them.
  • Your settings, such as reminder time and Hide titles, are kept.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its data from your phone. Backup files you exported stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Children

Tickora is a general-audience utility. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes

If a future version handles data differently (for example, adding widgets, voice input or optional syncing), we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
