Support

Tickora Daily (shown as “Tickora” on your Home Screen) helps you remember the dates that matter: passports, visas, car registration, insurance, warranties, memberships, home maintenance, and appointments.

Contact
Email support.tickora@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About). Don't send passport, card, or account numbers.

Getting started

  1. Tap Add Your First Deadline (or +).
  2. Enter a title, choose a category and the due date.
  3. Choose when to be reminded — on the due date, and optionally 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks, or 1 month before.
  4. Tap Add. The home screen shows what's next, what's overdue, and what's coming up.

Swipe a deadline right to mark it done, or left to edit or delete it. Use the category chips and the search button to find things quickly; use the filter menu to see completed items.

Reminders aren't appearing

  1. Open iOS Settings → Notifications → Tickora and make sure Allow Notifications is on.
  2. Check that a Focus mode or Scheduled Summary isn't holding them back.
  3. In Tickora, open the deadline and check Next reminder. If it says the reminder dates have passed, pick an earlier reminder or a later due date.
  4. Check the reminder time in Tickora's Settings (default 9:00 AM, your local time).
  5. Open Tickora once in a while. iOS limits how many reminders an app can schedule, so Tickora keeps the next 60 scheduled and adds more each time you open it.

Reminders are delivered by iOS and, like all notifications, can't be guaranteed — for example if your iPhone is off.

Where your data is stored

Only on your iPhone. Tickora has no account and no cloud sync, so deadlines don't appear on your other devices. They are included in your iPhone backups if backups are on. See the Privacy Policy for details.

To erase everything, open Tickora's Settings and tap Delete All Data. This can't be undone.

"Your deadlines couldn't be opened"

This appears if the saved file is damaged or was created by a newer version of Tickora. Nothing is deleted. Try again, or update the app. Start Fresh sets the damaged file aside (it stays on the device) and starts an empty list.

Known limitations

  • No syncing between devices and no sharing with family members.
  • No repeating deadlines yet — add the next date after you complete one.
  • iPhone only; English only.
